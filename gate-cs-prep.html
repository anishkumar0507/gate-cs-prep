MCQOne correct option. Wrong answer costs −1/3 (1-mark) or −2/3 (2-mark).
MSQOne or more options correct. Full marks only if you pick exactly the right set. No negative, no partial marks.
NATType a number on the virtual keypad. Answer is accepted within a range. No negative marking.

Daily routine that works

  1. Aaj → Revision: clear the questions due today first. They are the ones you got wrong 1, 3, 7, 15 or 30 days ago.
  2. Aaj → Today's PYQs: solve the day's set of real questions you have never seen. A wrong answer shows the official solution, then a step-by-step explanation of your exact mistake, and joins your revision list.
  3. Learn: for any topic that keeps going wrong, read the lesson and "How GATE asks it + traps", and clear doubts in the chat.
  4. GATE 2027 tab: check which topics carry the most marks and practise those PYQs topic by topic.
  5. Mock Test: every weekend sit one full official paper in 180 minutes, newest first, then study the subject-wise breakdown.
